# Competition and alternatives (stages 1 and 5)

An investor asks: how big are the players, what is their advantage, how hard is it to copy ours? A founder should ask the same earlier, with the customer's eyes: what do they do today instead of buying from us?

## Rule of three layers

| Layer | Definition | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Direct competitors | do the same thing | Yandex and Google (search) |
| Indirect competitors | solve the same problem differently | for streaming video: cinema, DVD shops, torrents |
| Status quo | do nothing, or use a spreadsheet / assistant / agency | "we keep it in Excel" |

**If you hear "we have no competitors": suspicious.** If there are no direct competitors there are indirect ones; if there are no indirect ones, there is probably no demand. The biggest competitor is often the status quo (a famous example: a streaming company's CEO said they compete with sleep).

## Method (about half a day)

1. List 8-15 alternatives from three sources: customers' answers to "what do you use now"; search and marketplaces; investor portfolios and industry lists.
2. Classify each: direct / indirect / status quo.
3. For the 5-8 most relevant fill the matrix:

| Alternative | Who it serves | Price and model | What it does well | Where it fails the customer | Cost to switch |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

4. Add a row for "do nothing" with its real annual cost to the customer.
5. Write the positioning in one sentence: for [segment] who [pain], we [outcome] unlike [main alternative] because [reason]. The sentence records the work (alternatives -> unique attributes -> value -> customers -> category, April Dunford's order); it does not replace it. Check it against the УТП drafted at S (`value-proposition.md`): the two must be consistent; if they conflict, rewrite the УТП from the alternatives and the customers' words.

## Three questions of the competitive lens

- **Advantage:** do we have one at all? How strong? Will it let us grow?
- **Durability:** how hard is it to copy? How long would it take a funded competitor?
- **Competitors:** how many players? How big? What is their advantage? Direct or indirect?

Durability sources (canon): proprietary data that improves with use; integrations and switching cost; network effects; regulation or licences; distribution; brand and trust; cost advantage from scale or from technology. A feature that a competitor can ship in a sprint is not an advantage.

## Pricing sanity check

Compare your price per outcome with the price of the best alternative per outcome, not with list prices. Status quo cost sets the ceiling; the best direct competitor sets the reference.

## Gates

- Stage 1: at least three alternatives named by customers; the status quo cost estimated.
- Stage 5: positioning and durability written; a plausible answer to "why will we win in 18 months".
- Stop or Recycle if the status quo is "good enough" and nobody pays to leave it.

## Traps

- Comparing only feature lists; customers compare outcomes and effort.
- Ignoring the status quo and incumbents' bundles.
- Calling a lack of Google results "no competition".
- Treating a big competitor's slow roadmap as durable advantage.
